Quick answers

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. We pull the permit, schedule the inspection and are there when the inspector arrives. It is included in our quote.

Is it safe to keep resetting a tripped breaker?

Reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off and call us. Repeated tripping can mean an overloaded circuit or a fault.

Do you charge extra at night or on weekends?

No. Emergency calls have the same flat prices as daytime visits.

Are your electricians licensed?

Yes. Every electrician is state licensed, background checked, insured and drug tested.
